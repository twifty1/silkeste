Silkeste - Madeline from Celeste in Hollow Knight: Silksong

Needs your own copies of Hollow Knight: Silksong and Celeste. Melty installs everything else.
Madeline's art and sounds are read from your Celeste install when the game starts.

Controls (keyboard): WASD move, Space jump, mouse dash, Shift grab. F1 settings, F3 records, 1-8 emotes (right stick on a controller).

2.1.0: Much smoother menus, title screen and cutscenes (no more slow-motion cutscenes). New-file menu (Pack for the climb): game mode, difficulty and optional starting abilities (Cling Grip, Faydown Cloak, Drifter's Cloak, Clawline, Silk Soar; any on makes it an assist save). Sandbox stays on a file until it's deleted; deleting a file resets it. Difficulty in the pause menu. With two dashes, one hit leaves the other dash free, two hits in a row lock it twice as long. 8 emotes on keys 1-8 or the right stick, with an Emotes menu to see and rebind them. Customizing: headwear, companions from Celeste, aura, footprints and dash trail shapes, shown in the preview. Hair drawn into Celeste's sprites now matches your colours, and her hair shows her most dashes in cutscenes. Records moves to F3, Reset Splits to F5; the Tutorial and Timer keys are gone. Light Cutscenes option for slow PCs.
2.0.3: The game no longer closes by itself at the start: the Quit game? question stays on No while it opens, the mouse no longer picks Yes just by resting over it (click it instead), and a press that lands as it opens is ignored.
2.0.2: The download now includes the license files (LICENSE.txt for Silkeste, THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt for NVorbis and python-fsb5). Madeline's sleeping bag on the loading screen only comes out at night, and the Records scroll arrow sits clear of the list.
2.0.1: Menu sounds play again after you've been in a save (title, pause, Settings, Records), and the DEFEATED / GOLDEN stamp on a boss's picture no longer hides under the Rematch button.
2.0.0: Boss rematches: fight 30+ bosses again from Records (your save is backed up and put back exactly; a rematch death costs nothing; Give Up in the pause menu, Rematch last boss on the pause card). Game modes (Standard, Speedrunner, Sandbox) and difficulty (Story, Easy, Normal, Hard, Hardcore), kept per save and picked on the file select. Records overhaul: act filters, DEFEATED/GOLDEN stamps, area rings, a Run timeline page, photo frames and Almost there on Achievements. New file select (live Madeline, mode badges, Settings/Skins/Game Mode buttons), title screen that follows your clock and last area (Settings > Video), tips, campfire that wakes up by day. Madeline plays Silk Soar (Celeste spin) and the Needolin, hits Father of the Flame's braziers, reacts to bosses and low health. Ten new achievements, settings search (Ctrl+F), deaths in this room, new tutorial card.
1.9.0: One Settings screen for everything: Silksong's Game, Audio, Video and Controls pages sit beside Silkeste's Customization, Assist, Variants, Challenges, Speedrun and About tabs (Esc in game opens the pause menu: Continue, Settings, Quit). Quick Respawn (room entrance or bench), Records Postcards and Photos pages, eight new looks, restyled key binding pages, and a pause info card.
1.8.9: Same gameplay additions as 1.8.8: movement-reactive hair, optional landing dust (Appearance > Landing Effects), and reduced hair-update allocations. No low-detail menus option.
