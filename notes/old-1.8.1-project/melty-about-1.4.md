Celeste's Madeline takes Hornet's place in Hollow Knight: Silksong. She has her real sprites, animations, hair and sounds, and she moves exactly like she does in Celeste. All of it is read from your own copy of Celeste while you play, and nothing from Celeste is included in the download, so **you need Celeste installed**.

### How she plays
- **Your attack is your dash.** It goes in 8 directions and replaces the needle. Each dash hits an enemy once for a needle hit (one and a half against bosses), with no knockback.
- **You can't get hit while dashing.** You pass straight through enemies.
- **Celeste tech works:** supers, hypers, wavedashes, ultras and wall bounces. Jumping out of a dash keeps hitting enemies until you land, so try mixing them.
- **Don't spam dash on enemies!** Hitting an enemy locks your dash for 0.4 seconds, and her hair turns blue until it's back.
- Dash down onto spikes to bounce off them, and through balloons and bounce flowers to get your dash back.
- Hold down while falling to fast-fall.

### Progression
- **Cling Grip** unlocks grabbing and climbing walls, with Celeste's stamina.
- **Faydown Cloak** unlocks a second dash, and her hair turns pink while she has it.
- **Drifter's Cloak** lets her glide with Celeste's jellyfish: hold Jump while falling, and ride updrafts up.
- **Swift Step** makes her dash stronger: dashes go 25% further and the lock after hitting an enemy is 25% shorter.
- Healing (binding), tools, Clawline, Silk Soar and the map all work as usual, so the whole game stays beatable.

### Boss cards and golden berries
When a boss falls, Celeste's chapter card slides in with the fight's deaths, time, dashes, hits and heals. **Beat a boss on your first try, or later without healing, to earn its golden berry**, and the card turns gold. After a death, only a healless win counts. Assist Mode means no berry, and each boss gives one berry across all your saves. While it's still possible, the golden berry sits in the corner of the screen.

### Assist Mode
Press **F1** (or hold Taunt on a controller) for Settings: the speedrun timer, skins, controls, and Celeste's Assist Mode with game speed, infinite stamina, extra air dashes, dash assist and invincibility.

### Celeste touches
- The dash freezes the game for a split second, with Celeste's screen shake and slash.
- Footsteps and wall sounds change with the ground, and dust kicks up as you move.
- When you die you get Celeste's death burst and screen wipe, and she respawns the Celeste way.
- Benches work like campfires: she dozes off, then wakes up and stands when you leave.
- Healing turns her into Celeste's glowing gold feather star.
- Stand still and she does Celeste's idle animations; she sits down to play the Needolin.
- In cutscenes she acts out Hornet's moments with Celeste animations.
- When Hornet speaks in conversations, you hear Madeline's Celeste voice.
- Celeste's skull death counter is kept for each save file, and **F3** shows Celeste's speedrun timer.

### Skins (F2)
Madeline, Badeline, the tutorial Ghost, Celeste Classic's PICO-8 Madeline, and five palettes: Snowbound, Core, Reflection, Forest and Pharloom. Pick one by name in the F1 settings, or press F2. Turn her backpack off in Settings too.

### Controls
**A controller is recommended.** The first time you play, Silkeste sets these controls once; rebind them afterwards as you like.

- **Controller:** R1 dash, R2 grab and climb, Cross jump, Square tools, Circle heal, Triangle Needolin, L2 Clawline.
- **Keyboard:** left mouse dash, Shift grab and climb, Space jump, WASD move, right mouse tools, E heal, F Needolin, Q Clawline.
- **F1** Settings, **F2** skin, **F3** speedrun timer, **F4** Tutorial. All four can be changed under Controls in Settings.

A Tutorial postcard shows each time you load a save, and another postcard explains each unlock when you get it.

Solo only. Have fun!
