# Silkeste

Celeste's Madeline replaces Hornet in Hollow Knight: Silksong. Her sprites, animations, hair and sounds are
read from your own copy of Celeste while the game runs; nothing from Celeste is included in this download.

Solo only (version 1.8.0).

## Controls

| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Dash (8 directions) | Attack (your old needle button) |
| Grab / climb (hold) | Dash / Sprint |
| Jump | Jump |
| Move / aim the dash | Move |
| Settings (Assist Mode, timer, looks, controls) | F1, or hold Taunt (R3) on a controller |
| Next saved look | F2 |
| Cycle the speedrun timer | F3 |
| Show controls and what you've unlocked | F4 |
| Records (bosses, areas, golden berries, achievements) | F5, or Share / Create on a PlayStation controller |
| Theo's camera (a photo without the HUD) | F6, or L3 on a controller |

The F keys can be changed under Controls in the settings. Settings and Records also work with the mouse (hover, click, scroll); using a controller hides the cursor again.

A controller is recommended. The first time you play Silkeste it sets its default controls, once; rebind them
afterwards as you like.

- Controller (PlayStation / Xbox): Cross / A jump, R1 / RB dash, R2 / RT grab and climb, Square / X tools (with up
  or down for the others), Circle / B heal (bind), Triangle / Y Needolin, L2 / LT Clawline, L1 / LB quick map,
  R3 taunt.
- Keyboard: WASD move and aim, Space jump, left mouse dash, Shift grab and climb, E heal (bind), F Needolin,
  Q Clawline, right mouse tools (with W or S for the others). Map and inventory keys stay as they were, except
  the tools menu, which moves from Q to R.

Binding, tools, Clawline, Silk Soar and the map work as usual, so the game stays beatable.

The game explains itself with Celeste's postcards: a Tutorial card each time you load a save, as soon as
you can move; one right after you pick up Cling Grip, Faydown Cloak or Drifter's Cloak (Silksong's own message still describes
Hornet's move); and the Tutorial again whenever you press F4.

## How Madeline plays

- Celeste's movement, using the numbers from Celeste's own player code: run, variable jump, coyote time,
  8-way dash, wall jump, grab and climb with stamina, and climbing over ledges.
- Celeste tech works: supers, hypers, wavedashes, ultras and wall bounces. Hold down while falling to fast-fall:
  you fall faster and Madeline stretches like in Celeste.
- **The dash is your attack.** It replaces the needle. One dash hit deals one needle hit (your current
  needle upgrade; one and a half against bosses, since she can't dash as often as Hornet swings), gives silk like a needle hit and causes no knockback.
- **Dash through enemies.** You take no enemy damage during the dash and a moment after it, and each enemy
  can be hit once per dash. Hitting an enemy does not give your dash back: it refills when you touch the
  ground, like in Celeste. After any enemy hit you can't dash again for 0.4 seconds; Madeline's hair stays
  blue until you can, then flashes.
- **Dash jumps hit too.** Jump out of a dash (super, hyper, wavedash or wall bounce) and the jump keeps
  hitting enemies and keeps you safe from enemy damage until you land.
- Every dash has Celeste's feel: a split-second freeze, a small screen shake and the slash streak.
- Celeste's footsteps, landings, wall grabs, handholds and wall-slide sound change with the ground of each
  area (stone, dirt, wood, metal, grass, snow, cloth), and Celeste's dust puffs when you jump, land, wall
  jump or slide.
- After a death or hazard hit she respawns like in Celeste: the death burst gathers back into her with the
  revive sound. At a bench she sits down and dozes off like at Celeste's campfire, and wakes up and stands
  when you leave. Stand still for a few seconds and she does Celeste's idle moves (scratching, sneezing;
  Badeline cracks her knuckles).
- **Healing:** when you bind, Madeline curls up into Celeste's glowing gold feather star, then bursts back
  out in gold feathers with Celeste's feather sound as the heal lands. She hangs still while she heals, even in the air.
- **Death counter:** Celeste's skull counter shows in the top-right after every death and while you rest
  at a bench. Only real deaths count (spikes or lava that just send you back don't), separately for each
  save slot.
- **Spikes:** after a downward or diagonal-down dash you bounce off spikes just before you touch them, with
  Silksong's clang, and your dash refills. It works until you land, even when the dash ended a while before
  the spikes. Any other spike touch still hurts, and acid, lava and the like always hurt.
- Levers, breakable walls, vines, chests, grass and Mossberry bushes react to the dash like the needle.
- Celeste's corner correction: jump or dash into the edge of a ceiling and you slip past it instead of bonking.
- Climbing has Celeste's stamina: Madeline flashes red when she is about to slip off.
- Room changes keep your momentum (Celeste's rules) and you get control right at the doorway.
- Dying plays Celeste's death burst and Chapter 1's screen wipe; at benches Madeline sits down.

## Assist Mode

Celeste's Assist Mode, with Celeste's own options. Press F1 (or hold Taunt on a controller for a second); the game stops while the menu is open, and your choices are kept. The settings have four tabs (Assist, Appearance, Speedrun, Controls): switch with L1/R1 on a controller or Q/E on a keyboard. The hints at the bottom show PlayStation buttons when you use a controller.

- **Game Speed:** 50% to 100%. Everything slows down, enemies and bosses too.
- **Infinite Stamina:** climb and hold onto walls as long as you like.
- **Air Dashes:** Default, Two or Infinite dashes before landing.
- **Dash Assist:** time stops while you hold dash and an arrow shows where you'll go, so you can aim.
- **Invincibility:** enemies, spikes and hazards can't hurt you (bottomless pits still send you back).

## Boss cards and golden berries

When a boss falls, Celeste's chapter card slides in with the fight's deaths, time, dashes, hits taken and heals. Beat a boss on your first try (no deaths to it on this save) and you get a **golden berry**, healing or not; after a death, the berry needs a fight without healing (no binding), one per boss across all your saves; the card turns gold. At the start of the fight the boss's golden berry follows you like in Celeste, then moves to a small icon in the top-right corner; once you've died to that boss, binding makes it laugh and fly away. Fights with Assist Mode on carry Celeste's Assist Mode stamp and don't earn berries.

## Speedrun timer

Celeste's speedrun timer at the top left, under your masks and silk, once the Tutorial card is closed, set in the F1 settings or cycled with F3: this save's time, all saves together, the time in this room, or a save time with the room time under it. It only runs while you can move (not during postcards, menus, cutscenes or benches).

## Progression

Madeline's abilities follow your Silksong save:

| You have | Madeline can |
|---|---|
| From the start | Dash (your attack), and a boost up onto ledges you just miss |
| Cling Grip | Grab and climb walls with stamina, and wall jump |
| Faydown Cloak | Dash twice before landing (pink hair, Celeste's two-dash sound) |
| Drifter's Cloak | Glide with Celeste's jellyfish: hold Jump while falling; updrafts lift you |
| Swift Step | A longer dash (1.25x as far) and a shorter lock after hitting an enemy (0.3 s instead of 0.4 s); she dashes from the start without it |

## Looks

Under Appearance in the F1 settings you build your own look, with a live preview on the right (click the arrows
to change, Tab or Square for a pose, R or Triangle resets the look). Four looks are saved; F2 switches between them.

- **Skin:** Madeline, Badeline, the Knight (Hollow Knight in Celeste pixels: horned mask, cloak, Focus heal,
  shade dash, soul wisps when out of dashes) or Pink Cat.
- **Outfit:** Madeline's clothes in Snowbound, Core, Reflection, Forest, Pharloom or Pink Cat colours.
- **Cat Ears:** ears and a tail in her hair colour.
- **Hair:** a colour for two dashes, one dash and no dash (29 colours), a length (Short to Very Long), a volume (Thin, Normal, Fluffy) and an effect (Rainbow, Light Tips, Dark Tips).
- **Backpack:** on or off.

When she speaks in a conversation you hear Celeste's dialogue babble (Madeline's, or Badeline's) instead of
Hornet's voice, and Hornet's hurt cries are silent.

## Tools

Some Silksong tools change Madeline's dash:

- **Longclaw:** the dash reaches as much further as Longclaw lengthens Hornet's needle (1.2x).
- **Pin Badge** and an active **Flea Brew:** 0.05 s off the lock after hitting an enemy each (Swift Step: 0.1 s).
- **Flea Brew** and **Silkspeed Anklets:** run 33% faster each, 50% with both.
- **Sawtooth Circlet:** gliding with the jellyfish hurts what you touch.
- **Memory Crystal:** a crystal Madeline stays where you were hit.

## Silk skills

Silk skills still do what they do for Hornet, and Madeline adds a Celeste move: Thread Storm gives every dash back,
Sharpdart is a long piercing dash straight ahead, and Rune Rage launches her like Celeste's red booster where you aim.

## Theo's camera

F6 (or L3) takes a photo without the HUD and saves it to Pictures\Silkeste, with a polaroid of it in the corner.
Beating a boss takes one by itself, with the boss card in the shot.

## Records

F5 opens the records: every boss beaten with its best run (on this save or all saves, with its Hunter's Journal
picture), every area with its deaths and time, and which bosses still need their golden berry. The first time
you enter an area on a save, Celeste's chapter card shows its name.

The Achievements tab holds 22 achievements in four tiers (Easy green, Medium orange, Hard red, Farewell purple)
and five categories, shown as glowing squares: hover or move to one to see how to get it, sort by difficulty or
category, and reset one or all of them (Square or R; resetting everything has to be held). Unlocking one plays
a banner with Celeste's sounds; getting every other one unlocks The Summit. Achievements count across all saves.
Moss Grotto and Moss Mother don't count for the combat and golden berry ones.
## Celeste-style menus and text boxes

Every menu looks like Celeste's: the title screen (with SILKESTE and Celeste's menu icons), Options and all its pages, the keyboard and controller pages (keys as Celeste's pills), the quit prompts and the pause menu, over a painted Celeste mountain sky, with Celeste's sounds and smooth transitions. The save files are Celeste's file cards (portrait, area, golden berries, deaths and time on the ticket) with a Begin menu. Conversations use Celeste's text box: letters hop in, names and places in their own colours, the speaker's name on a tab, Madeline's lines in a warmer tint. Yes/no questions (shops, paying rosaries) use a Celeste box with Celeste's choice menu. On the title, Achievements opens the Records' achievements and Extra Settings opens the settings.

## Requirements

- Hollow Knight: Silksong (the game it plays in), with BepInEx 5 (Melty installs it).
- Celeste (PC). Melty passes its folder to the mod.

On first launch the mod reads Madeline's sounds straight from Celeste's sound banks (a few seconds, in the background),
then renders Celeste's exact versions once with its CelesteSfx helper where Windows allows it, all into
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\MadelineInPharloom\cache`.
