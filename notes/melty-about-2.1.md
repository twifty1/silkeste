**Play all of Hollow Knight: Silksong as Madeline from Celeste.** Her real sprites, hair, sounds and movement, dashing and climbing through Pharloom.

🍓 **Free, one click.** You need Silksong and Celeste. Madeline is read from your own copy of Celeste, and nothing from it is in the download.

### Why play it
- **Celeste movement in a whole new world:** 8-way dash, climbing, supers, hypers and wavedashes.
- **Your dash is your attack.** Dash through enemies and bosses, and nothing hits you mid-dash.
- **Golden berries:** beat a boss first try, or without healing, to earn its berry.
- **Boss rematches:** fight 30+ bosses again from your Records.
- **Make her yours:** 11 skins, 29 hair colours, hats, a Celeste companion (the bird, berries, a crystal heart...), auras and emotes.
- **Feels like Celeste:** the title screen, menus and save files look like Celeste's, conversations use Celeste's text boxes, dying plays Celeste's death burst and respawn, and resting at a bench is like sitting by Celeste's campfire.

### New in 2.1
- Much smoother menus and cutscenes.
- Pick your mode, difficulty and **starting abilities** when you start a file.
- **8 emotes** on keys 1-8 or the right stick.
- Headwear, companions, auras, footprints and dash trail shapes.

### Controls
🎮 **A controller is recommended.** Each line shows controller · keyboard.

- **Dash / attack:** R1 · left mouse
- **Jump:** Cross · Space
- **Grab and climb:** R2 · Shift
- **Settings:** hold Taunt · F1
- **Records:** Share · F3
- **Emotes:** right stick · 1-8

Everything can be rebound in Settings.

### Good to know
- **Climbing** comes with Cling Grip, a **second dash** with the Faydown Cloak, and a **jellyfish glide** with the Drifter's Cloak. You can also start with them.
- **Don't spam your dash:** a hit locks it for a moment, and her hair turns blue until it's back.
- **Difficulty** runs from Story to Hardcore, plus Celeste's Assist Mode, Variants and Challenges for silly or brutal runs.
- **Extras:** 32 achievements, a speedrun timer with splits, and Theo's camera (F6).
